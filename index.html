Better to Reign — The Flow of Malsheem

The opera-citadel as a bounded dungeon: nine tiers, one per layer of Baator, ~86 rooms, from front-of-house up to the Throne. Up is down — the party climbs the house and descends into Hell, so the page reads top (arrival, Avernus) to bottom (Nessus, the Throne). Room IDs and fragment modes match the card deck. Each tier: an entry, five fragment rooms, two traversal rooms, the Principal's stage, the ascent.

Front-of-house Entry / ascent Fragment room Traversal Principal (mini-boss) The Throne

Fragment modes: [C] combat · [P] puzzle · [R] riddle (answer tied to the room). Edges: ——— passage · · frag · a fragment feeds the Principal's stage · ══▶ the ascent to the next tier.

How to read it. Work each tier: collect its five fragments (they feed the Principal's stage, dotted), assemble the tier's key, then take the Principal and climb. The win-gate meter in the Ledger-House (A2) tracks how many Principals have fallen; enough breaks the performance and opens the Throne. Pull a room's card for its read-aloud and run-stats; this is the skeleton.
